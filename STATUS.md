## Still open / honest limits

- **No hardware built or measured.** Everything is simulation and literature, not a device. The 2D model is an infinite extrusion (true 3D field is ~25% stronger near the magnet, decays as 1/r³ not 1/r²); static only (no eddy currents, no thermal effects); simple saturation capping, not a full B–H curve (~1,300 cells remain modestly above their cap after the last pass); star tips are a 2D stand-in for the true in-plane star; forces are order-of-magnitude estimates.
- **Material values are assumptions, not measurements:** N52 grade (Br 1.45 T), cast-iron μ_r = 200, magnetic stainless μ_r = 100. **Resolved 2026-11-10: Gregory checked — the throwing star is magnetic, so the tip concentration and the well stand as simulated; the austenitic warning no longer applies to this star.**
- **Paint coupon test planned, not run:** plan written (see above); no panels built, no measurements made, nothing bought. Bulk ferrite and GNP prices now verified (see the plan's §2 and Appendix A).
- **Nothing levitates the badge.** A levitating version would need a far larger susceptibility×volume product or a different build.
